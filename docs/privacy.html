PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

Welcome to Thekedaar. We prioritize user data privacy. This policy describes how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Local-First Offline Architecture

All your day-to-day data (sites, daily attendance, material bills, vouchers, supplier ledgers) is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. We do not store your accounts on a central public server.

2. Google Drive Encrypted Sync

When you enable Google Drive backup, the app connects directly to your own Google account using Google's secure drive.file scope. The backup files and pictures are encrypted with AES-256 before upload. We never see your Google password or Drive files outside the Thekedaar folder.

3. Payment Processing via Razorpay

For Thekedaar Pro subscriptions (₹299/month), payments are processed directly by Razorpay Software Private Limited. We do not collect or store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs. Razorpay is fully PCI-DSS certified and governed by RBI norms.

4. Camera & Storage Permissions

Permissions are strictly used to capture receipt photos, vouchers, and materials challans. We never access your personal photo albums without permission.

5. No Third-Party Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your construction site details or financial records with any third-party marketing companies.

6. Contact for Privacy Matters

For inquiries, please write to support@thekedaar.app or visit our Contact Us page.
